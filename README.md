# Design and implimentation of a House Listing Website

A responsive web application for browsing and listing rental properties.
Problem being solved. This website focuses on solving suituations whereby  people walk around looking houses to rent or maybe buy and don't find houses of theirs choices or may spend an entire just for that still won't find a house of their preferences. Here arises a need for an online plaftform where they can comfortably do their research by using filter by catory and prices.

key features: save properties, filtering by price and category, login/register authentification, property listings, contact and inquiry.

Technology used: vscode,html, css, javascript,git/github,netlify.
